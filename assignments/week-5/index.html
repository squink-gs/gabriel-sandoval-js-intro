Gabriel Sandoval

About

Gabriel was born in El Salvador. At age seven, he migrated to the United States with his mother to reunite with his father, who had left El Salvador four years prior. He grew up in Koreatown in Los Angeles, California, and later moved to the Bay Area to attend UC Berkeley. Despite facing much adversity as a first-generation student, Gabriel graduated with a bachelor's degree in Data Science with an emphasis in science, technology, and society.

Through a variety of experiences and personal projects, Gabriel has found that his passions lie at the intersection of entrepreneurship and technology. He now aspires to become a digital solutions consultant working with clients to identify their challenges and develop tech solutions that help them better serve their communities. Through his work, he also hopes to inspire other first-generation individuals to pursue their dreams.

Gabriel currently works at Mujeres Unidas y Activas (MUA) as a DreamSF Fellow. At MUA, he supports the Data and Reporting team by collecting and analyzing data, assisting with outreach, and working on implementing any digital solutions to promote and facilitate personal transformation and community building for Latina and indigenous women.
